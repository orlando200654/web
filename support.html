Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my previous In-App Purchases?
Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google Play account used for the original purchase, open the app's Settings screen, and tap Restore Purchases.
How do I manage or cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are managed directly by your app store. On iOS, go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play > Profile Icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
How can I request deletion of my account or personal data?
You can delete your account directly inside the app settings (where applicable) or submit a formal erasure request through our Data Deletion Request page.
