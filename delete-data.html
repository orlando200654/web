Account & Data Deletion

Instructions and request portal for deleting user accounts and associated data.

How to Request Data Deletion

Users of applications published by can request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data using either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion (Where Applicable): Open the application, navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account, and confirm your request.
  2. Web / Email Request: Complete the deletion request form below or send an email to with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted Data: User profile details, authentication identifiers, cloud progress/saved states, and support correspondence linked to your account will be permanently erased within 30 days of verification.
  • Retained Data: Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics logs or financial transaction records required for tax, fraud prevention, or legal compliance by Apple App Store / Google Play Store may be retained only as required by applicable law.
