Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Eastlake Pom ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile applications, games, and websites available through the Google Play Store, Apple App Store, or related digital platforms (collectively, the "Services").

By downloading, installing, or using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

Depending on the features of a specific application, we may collect limited information necessary to provide, maintain, and improve our Services:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: Information you submit directly when contacting customer support or requesting assistance (such as your name or email address).
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Non-personal technical diagnostics such as device model, operating system version, language preference, crash logs, and performance metrics.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iOS, subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission where applicable) used for analytics, fraud prevention, or displaying advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our applications may integrate trusted third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, authentication, in-app purchases, or advertising. These third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Firebase Crashlytics
  • Apple App Store Services

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Our applications do not generally use browser cookies directly. However, third-party code, SDKs, or web views embedded in the Services may use cookies, local storage, or similar identifiers to remember preferences and improve service quality. You can manage tracking and ad personalization settings within your mobile device's system settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain collected data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. You may request deletion of any personal or account data associated with you at any time via our Data Deletion Request page.

6. Security

We value your trust in using our Services and implement commercially reasonable administrative and technical safeguards (including encrypted HTTPS/TLS transmission) to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) without verifiable parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can promptly remove such data.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA / Local Laws)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, opt out of personalized advertising, or request erasure of your personal data. Where required by law (such as in the EEA/UK or on iOS 14.5+), we request your consent before collecting data for personalized advertising.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
